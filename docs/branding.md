# Branding

## Direction

The accepted branding direction combines:

- a culturally resonant mythological or scientific name; and
- provocative surveillance-oriented messaging that creates an immediate reaction.

The public identity should feel powerful and unsettling without representing the software as covert or deceptive.

## Current shortlist

### Panoptes

**Status:** leading platform-name candidate.

Panoptes means “all-seeing” and is associated with Argus Panoptes, the hundred-eyed guardian in Greek mythology.

Why it fits:

- many data sources become many “eyes”;
- the ontology unifies those observations;
- it evokes surveillance and intelligence;
- it supports a strong eye-based visual identity; and
- it is more distinctive than Argus.

Candidate lines:

- **See what sees you.**
- **The all-seeing system you own.**
- **All your data. One eye.**
- **Install your own watcher.**

### Laplace

**Status:** leading inference-engine candidate and possible product-name candidate.

The name refers to Laplace's Demon: a hypothetical intelligence that could predict the future if it knew the complete state of the present.

Why it fits:

- it directly represents inference and prediction;
- it provides an intellectual explanation for “Predict My Monday”;
- it distinguishes observation from forecasting; and
- it can name the engine inside Panoptes.

Candidate lines:

- **Your past knows what comes next.**
- **Give it enough data. Ask what happens next.**
- **Build the demon already hiding in your data.**

## Provisional naming hierarchy

```text
Panoptes — complete personal intelligence platform
└── Laplace — inference, forecasting, and experiment engine
```

This hierarchy is provisional. Both names remain candidates until collision and trademark research is completed.

## Messaging strategy

### Primary credible statement

> Panoptes is a consent-based personal intelligence system that shows what your data reveals and turns it into useful predictions.

### Provocative hooks

- **Your personal malware. This time, you're the operator.**
- **Big Brother already has your file. Build your own.**
- **Your data knows what you'll do next. Shouldn't you?**
- **A hundred eyes already watch you. These ones work for you.**
- **The spyware idea—without the spying.**

Provocative copy should immediately resolve into a transparent explanation of consent, ownership, and local-first processing. The contrast is the message: malware observes for someone else; Panoptes observes for the participant.

## Tone

The desired tone is:

- culturally literate;
- technical;
- powerful;
- unsettling enough to invite curiosity;
- direct rather than euphemistic; and
- credible under engineering scrutiny.

Avoid a purely wellness-oriented visual identity. The product should feel like a personal intelligence and operations platform.

## Names and phrases to avoid as formal product names

- **Palantir/Gotham:** existing company and product identities
- **Sauron/Eye of Sauron:** protected modern franchise association
- **Pegasus:** existing commercial spyware and associated organizations
- **Oculus:** existing Meta brand
- **Oracle:** existing major technology company
- **Skynet/Cerebro:** protected entertainment-franchise associations
- **Big Brother:** better used as cultural campaign language than the formal identity

## Before final selection

Perform and document searches across:

- GitHub organizations and repositories;
- package registries;
- Apple App Store and major software catalogs;
- domain names and social handles;
- relevant trademark databases; and
- search-engine results for security-product collisions.

A final name should be distinctive enough that the project can be found without adding “data tracker” to every search.

## Visual directions to explore

- many observations converging into one eye;
- a graph/constellation forming an iris;
- dual past/future faces for Laplace predictions;
- evidence paths illuminating from raw event to conclusion; and
- dark intelligence-system aesthetics with clear consent/status indicators.

Do not copy Palantir Gotham's interface, typography, iconography, or product screenshots.
